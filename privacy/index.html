Privacy Policy

Chandler Queenan · Last updated November 18, 2026

This policy covers the text messages Chandler Queenan sends to wedding guests, and the address page those messages link to.

What we collect

Your name and mobile number, which you gave us personally as a guest, and the mailing address you enter on our address page.

How we use it

Only to send you a text asking for your mailing address, and to mail you wedding stationery such as our save the date and invitation. We keep this information in a private list that only we can see.

Sharing

We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. Text messages are delivered through our messaging provider, Twilio, only so they can reach your phone.

Opting out

Reply STOP to any message to stop receiving texts. Reply HELP for help.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent by replying HELP to our text, or to Chandler Queenan directly.
